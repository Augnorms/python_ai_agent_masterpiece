Company Time Off Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per year. 
Leave requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal. 
Unused leave can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. 
If an employee is sick for more than 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note must be provided to the HR department.

Remote Work Policy

Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 2 days per week. 
Remote work requests must be approved by the direct manager. 
The company provides a $500 stipend for home office equipment.
